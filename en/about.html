About

Bresson’s Notes on Cinematography describe fairly well what I look for in cinematic art. I love films that employ the resources of cinematography and use the camera in order to create. For me, cinema is neither literature, nor filmed theatre, nor a photographer’s snapshot, but « a writing with images in movement and with sounds »: camera movement or its absence, framing and editing. These are what matter most — screenplay, photography and performance, however important, are in my view secondary. I prefer the cinema that observes to the cinema that explains, cinema as an art of reality rather than an art of the image, films that « invent time », as Daney says.
